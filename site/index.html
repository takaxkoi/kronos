How it works

From candles to a graded call.

Capture

After every close, prices and point-in-time market data are saved to the vault.

Simulate

Kronos reads recent candles and samples thirty possible paths for each stock.

Decide

Agreement, ranges, signals and trade plans are built from those paths.

Grade

When each forecast window closes, the call is scored against reality.

The point isn't to sound confident. It's to know, with numbers, when the model has earned your trust and when it hasn't.

  • Every call is recorded before the outcome is known.
  • Calibration is checked: 80% sure should mean right about 80% of the time.
  • Paper money first. Real-money automation stays locked until the report card passes.
  • Forecasts, not promises. Not financial advice.
